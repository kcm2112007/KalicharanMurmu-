About Me

I'm a student who genuinely enjoys the process of figuring things out — whether that's understanding how a website comes together, learning a new tool, or working through a difficult concept until it finally clicks. Technology and web development caught my attention early, and I've been building on that curiosity ever since, one small project at a time.

Alongside academics, I spend my time learning to code, experimenting with web projects, and exploring how digital products are built and shipped. I care about doing the work consistently rather than chasing shortcuts, because I believe steady, honest effort compounds into real skill over time.

I'm also preparing seriously for competitive opportunities while keeping an entrepreneurial mindset — looking for ways to create value, solve real problems, and eventually build something of my own. This portfolio is a living record of that journey: what I'm learning, what I'm building, and where I'm headed.
